I'm a Jr. Flutter Developer based in Indore, building mobile applications full-time while finishing my B.Tech in Computer Science.

My work leans toward apps that have to behave in the real world — fleet tracking, emergency reporting, event management. Networks fail. GPS drifts. Users tap the wrong button at the wrong time.
